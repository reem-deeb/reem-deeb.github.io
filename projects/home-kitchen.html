Overview

Real home cooking, from certified chefs near you.

Home Kitchen is a marketplace for home-cooked food. Customers follow certified home chefs, browse their dishes by category and order for delivery or pick-up — or pre-order a meal for a chosen day and time. Chefs get their own version of the app to publish dishes, set delivery options and manage incoming orders.

01

A house with a ladle

The logo draws a house whose chimney is a ladle, with steam rising from the bowl: cooking that happens at home. A warm, appetising yellow carries the brand, with a red accent reserved for prices and offers.

04 — The customer journey

From craving to doorstep

  1. Step 01

    Sign in

    A light login over a hand-drawn kitchen pattern, with Google and Facebook one tap away.

  2. Step 02

    Discover

    The best chefs, food categories and trending dishes on one scrolling home screen.

  3. Step 03

    Meet the chef

    Every chef has a profile with followers, a short bio and their menu, sorted by cuisine.

  4. Step 04

    Choose a meal

    Ingredients, delivery time and add-ons — plus a date and time picker for pre-orders.

  5. Step 05

    Cart

    Quantities, subtotal and delivery fee, then a choice: pick it up or have it delivered.

  6. Step 06

    Address & payment

    A three-step checkout keeps it calm: saved addresses first, then cash on delivery or card.

  7. Step 07

    Order placed

    A friendly confirmation with the order number — and the order can be tracked right away.

05

The chef version

The same app, turned around for the people behind the stove: chefs run their profile, publish dishes with prices and delivery options, and follow every order from pending to on the way.

Publish in minutes

One form covers a dish’s photo, price, description, delivery time, add-ons and allergens — and whether it can be pre-ordered.

Orders at a glance

Active and previous orders are split into two tabs, each with a clear status: pending, processing or on the way.
