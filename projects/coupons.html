Overview

Enjoy more. Save more.

Coupons App helps people discover, collect and redeem discount deals and promo codes from the stores they love. I created the brand and a clean, user-friendly UI/UX with a vibrant identity, focused on seamless navigation from browsing categories to redeeming offers — plus a seller version for stamping coupons in store and an admin dashboard to run it all.

01

From research to release

The project ran in six phases. Mapping every function first meant the wireframes — and later the interface — had a clear structure to grow into.

  1. AnalysisGoals, users and competitors
  2. Interviews & structureUser needs into an app map
  3. PrototypingLow-fidelity wireframes
  4. Design systemColours, type, icons, components
  5. Interface designEvery screen, high fidelity
  6. PresentationMockups and hand-off
02

A bag full of savings

The mark blends a shopping bag with a price tag and a cart, in a fresh green that reads as “save” at a glance. A warm yellow highlights rewards and stamps.

03 — The app

Collect, track, redeem

  1. Step 01

    Sign in

    A dark, focused entry screen with the brand front and centre — and account creation one tap away.

  2. Step 02

    Your coupons

    Stamp cards show progress at a glance — “buy 9 coffees, get the 10th free” — with categories and the latest offers right below.

  3. Step 03

    Explore a store

    Every store has a profile with its social links, current offers and the coupons you are collecting there.

  4. Step 04

    Redeem an offer

    Offer pages put the deal, its deadline and related offers in one clear view, ready to redeem.

04

Behind the counter

A seller version lets staff scan a customer’s QR code and stamp their card in seconds, while the admin dashboard manages stores, offers and coupons, with live numbers at a glance.
