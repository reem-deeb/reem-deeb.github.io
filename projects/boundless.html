Overview

A channel for ideas without limits.

Boundless Box is a tutorial channel about architecture — videos on engineering software like 3ds Max, essential tips and a gallery of the best designs. The identity combines high-tech geometric shapes with vibrant light to give the channel a modern, authoritative and engaging presence across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

01

A box with open corners

A personal brand for a channel that teaches architecture and 3D visualisation, built around one simple, open shape.

The name

Chosen to express the idea of unlimited ideas, especially in the architectural field: learning, sharing and designing without limits.

The mark

A box whose corners are left open, a visual sign of distinctive ideas that live outside the box. One face is filled with light, like a model coming to life in the viewport.

02

Light on dark

Two luminous mints glow against near-black, like light tracing the edges of a 3D model, while a soft grey keeps text calm and readable.
