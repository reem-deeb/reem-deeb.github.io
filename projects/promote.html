Overview

Where creators and local venues meet.

PROMOTE is an end-to-end influencer marketing ecosystem. A mobile app lets bloggers and content creators discover local restaurants, apply for campaigns and manage their bookings; a web-based Business Portal lets owners publish promotions, review applications and run their campaign calendar. I designed both sides so that every step — for the creator and for the venue — is clear, quick and trustworthy.

01 — The creator journey

From discovery to reward

  1. Step 01

    Discover

    Featured and nearby promotions in a list or on the map, filtered by what suits the creator’s audience.

  2. Step 02

    Apply

    Each promotion lays out the brief, the incentive and the content required. A few questions and a platform choice later, the application is in.

  3. Step 03

    Book

    Approved creators choose a date and time that suits the venue, then manage, reschedule or cancel from one place.

  4. Step 04

    Visit & redeem

    At the venue, a QR code confirms the booking with staff in seconds — no awkward explanations at the counter.

  5. Step 05

    Deliver & earn

    Upload the content, follow its review, and watch bonus targets fill up as the views grow.

02

Every state, designed

Onboarding, filters, counter offers, late cancellations, missed visits, edits required and password resets — the unhappy paths are designed as carefully as the happy ones, so creators always know what happens next.
