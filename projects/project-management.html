Overview

Every task, every hour, every person — in one place.

The app helps construction teams manage projects, speed up their work and communicate better. Managers create tasks and assign people to them; everyone who joins a project sees its tasks and records when they start and finish. At any moment you can see who is working on what — and how long each task took.

Built-in reports show how much time the project and each task took, and how many hours every team member worked in a chosen period.

01

The main problems

Conversations with managers and builders pointed to the same three gaps in how construction work was organised.

  • There is no organisation among those in charge of the work, and no professional communication between the workers.
  • No one knows how far the workers have got with the tasks assigned to them.
  • Files needed for the work cannot be organised, and not everyone has access to them.
02

Sprint design

The project followed a five-step design sprint, keeping the users at the centre from the first interview to the final test.

  1. EmpathizeUnderstanding the users and their problems, to build a process completely centred on their needs.
  2. DefineDefining the main outlines of the software and its purpose, and how they shape the design and architecture.
  3. IdeateTurning the outlines into ideas that can be achieved in both design and development.
  4. PrototypeThe first working prototype, built in high fidelity and ready for a user demo.
  5. TestThe prototype goes to user testing, so it can be refined before it is sent to development.
03

User needs

Turned around, the problems became three clear needs for the app to answer.

  • Organise projects and tasks for everyone working in the company.
  • Let managers upload every file a project needs, and decide who can access it.
  • Add as many projects and tasks as managers want — and an unlimited number of employees, each with their position.
04

User personas

Two people represent the two sides of every construction project: the one who plans the work and the one who builds it.

Bio

A project manager at the company for five years, with a Master’s degree in Business Administration from the United States.

Goals

Organise projects, explain them professionally and assign them to workers.

Add employees easily, with their private and important information.

Know how quickly each employee completes tasks — and where and why delays happen.

Problem statement

He cannot organise workers and tasks well, cannot see what they have achieved, and when there is a delay he does not know the reason. His primary goal is effective organisation.

Bio

A construction worker who has been with the company for more than two years. He works in the construction workshops and leads many other workers.

Goals

Know the tasks assigned to him without having to ask the manager.

Access every required file easily, in the same place as the tasks.

Problem statement

When a worker is delayed it causes chaos, because no one knows where the delay comes from — and he has to go to the manager to find out the latest developments.

08

Usability testing

I tested the prototype with users from the target group. They used the app with ease and liked the design, and their feedback shaped the next round of improvements.

Requested features

  • A calculator inside the app, as an additional feature.
  • A conversation feature so workers can talk to each other.

Pain points & feedback

  • The schedule is good, but needs more detail.
  • Users were happy with the interaction.
  • Users also liked the clean design.

Less chasing. More building.
