Overview

Quiet luxury, crafted strand by strand.

AN Beauty Salon specialises in luxury wigs and hair extensions made from 100% human hair. The identity had to feel as soft and refined as the product itself — calm, feminine and quietly confident, so every client feels she is in expert hands from the very first touchpoint.

01

The Monogram

The letters A and N are drawn as one flowing gesture: a fine, fashion-inspired stroke that recalls a single strand of hair. Framed by the words “Beauty Salon” on a circular path, the mark works as a signature in print and as a seal on packaging.

Fine & feminine

High-contrast strokes and generous curves borrow from couture labels, giving the salon an elegance that feels personal rather than corporate.

One mark, many sizes

The circular lock-up reads clearly as a small stamp on a soap box, while the monogram alone holds its own on bottles, cups and fabric.

02

Colour

A palette taken from skin, silk and natural hair: warm sand and cream as the calm base, blush for softness, and a deep cocoa that grounds the brand and lends it a premium weight.

Soft by nature, luxurious by design.

03

Packaging

Serums, toner mists, argan oil and soaps share one calm system: cream and taupe containers, a centred monogram and light, airy type — a range that looks as cared-for as the hair it treats.

04

Touchpoints

From staff uniforms to cushions and takeaway cups, the monogram appears in small, considered doses — present enough to be remembered, never loud enough to compete with the client.
