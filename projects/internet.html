Overview

Own your digital identity.

INTERNET.com helps people claim a clean, memorable email address — no numbers, no compromises. I designed the visual identity and the complete web experience: a landing page that sells the idea, a four-step onboarding that gets users to their new address in minutes, and a calm account dashboard for everything that comes after.

A name that connects

The logo combines fluid geometric shapes with bold typography to convey seamless connectivity, accessibility and modern professionalism — on the web and inside the product.

A line that connects

One fluid stroke runs from the @ across the wordmark to the dot of “.com” — a simple picture of seamless connectivity.

Bold, friendly type

Chunky slab letterforms keep the brand confident and approachable, while a blue-to-violet gradient gives it a modern, digital glow.
