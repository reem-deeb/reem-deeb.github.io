Overview

Beauty — and everything beyond it.

Bloom & Beyond is an interior design and execution company based in Dubai. It brings clients’ visions of beautiful, functional spaces to life through a comprehensive and thoughtful process that promises an exceptional result. The identity had to feel as considered as the interiors themselves: calm, refined and timeless.

The Monogram

The name carries a beautiful meaning: the studio’s work is not limited to beauty, but goes beyond it — to quality craftsmanship and attention to detail. The monogram expresses exactly that, combining two letters B, one set behind the other.
